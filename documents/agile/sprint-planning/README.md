# Sprint planning

One document per sprint, covering the sprint goal, the committed scope, team
capacity, and any risks or dependencies called out at planning.

## Naming

`sprint-NN-planning.md` — e.g. `sprint-01-planning.md`. Zero-pad so sprints
sort correctly past nine.

## Suggested outline

- **Sprint number and dates**
- **Sprint goal** — one sentence on what success looks like
- **Committed scope** — stories pulled in, with links to `../backlog/`
- **Capacity** — who is available, and for how much of the sprint
- **Risks and dependencies** — anything that could block the goal
- **Definition of done** — if it differs from the team default

Outcomes and lessons go to [`../retrospectives/`](../retrospectives/) at the
end of the sprint, not here.
