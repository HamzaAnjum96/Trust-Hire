# Research

Evidence that informs product decisions — market landscape, user interviews,
competitor analysis.

## What belongs here

| Topic | Typical file |
| --- | --- |
| User interviews | `YYYY-MM-DD-interview-<participant-ref>.md` |
| Competitor analysis | `competitor-<name>.md` |
| Market sizing and landscape | `market-landscape.md` |
| Survey results | `YYYY-MM-DD-survey-<topic>.md` |

## Conventions

- **Separate observation from interpretation.** Record what participants said
  or did, then draw conclusions in a clearly marked section. The raw record
  stays useful long after a conclusion is overturned.
- **Do not commit personal data.** Refer to participants by a reference
  (`P3`, `participant-a`), and keep names, contact details, CVs, and recordings
  out of the repository entirely. This is a hiring product — treat candidate
  and interviewee data as sensitive by default.
- Cite sources with a date; market data goes stale quietly.
