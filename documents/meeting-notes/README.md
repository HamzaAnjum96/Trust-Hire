# Meeting notes

Dated notes and, more importantly, the decisions that came out of them.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md` — e.g. `2026-07-27-kickoff.md`. The date prefix keeps
the folder in chronological order.

## Suggested outline

- **Date, attendees, purpose**
- **Discussion** — the substance, kept brief
- **Decisions** — what was actually agreed, one per line
- **Actions** — each with a named owner and a due date

Decisions are the part worth writing down. If a meeting produced a decision
that shapes the product or architecture, also reflect it in the relevant
document under [`../product/`](../product/) or
[`../../code/`](../../code/) — notes are a record, not a specification, and
nobody will find it here later.

Standups and retros do not belong here: retro notes go to
[`../agile/retrospectives/`](../agile/retrospectives/).
