## Direction contract

THESIS: Ink and turmeric on lime-wash. The colours of a working street in
Pakistan, walls washed with lime, lettering in near-black ink, turmeric for
the one thing that matters now, used with the discipline of a sunlit
instrument rather than as decoration. It refuses the category default of a
white screen with one brand accent, and the AI defaults (cream and terracotta,
neon on black, tech blue).

OWN-WORLD: A cool lime-wash ground (#F3F5F7), white sheets, hairline lines,
near-black ink (#16202A) for text and for every filled action, turmeric
(#F2A900) only for selection and what is new or waiting, peacock (#0B6E6B)
for voice notes and nothing else, brick (#B8321E) for loss. The shell is ink:
an ink navigation bar and rail carrying a turmeric indicator. Type is
Atkinson Hyperlegible Next with tabular figures; Urdu in Noto Sans Arabic,
Nastaliq for display. Chunky pill buttons; 16px cards, 24px sheets; filled
rounded icons. Dark: a night-slate ground (#0E141A) where turmeric becomes
the action fill under ink text.

STORY: The visitor knows where everything is before noticing the world. What
they read first is work: where it is, what it pays, whether it is theirs. The
job in hand is unmistakable wherever it appears. Voice notes are never
confused with anything else.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Jobs on a phone. The map fills the screen under a white search
bar (Ask, avatar) and white filter chips. Ink pins with white glyphs; the
selected pin turmeric with its fare flag above and a turmeric wash for its
area; its preview card sits at the bottom above the ink navigation bar, whose
Post slot is a turmeric pill with an ink plus. The view switch is an ink pill
reading "16 jobs".

SIGNATURE MOVE: The job in hand glows turmeric everywhere it appears: its pin
and area on the map, its row in the list, its fare flag. Nothing else on
screen is turmeric except what is waiting for the person (offers, unread).

RAISES (disciplines taken from the dealt challengers, never their clothes):
- From the split-flap concourse: columns never move. Fares and times sit in
  fixed, right-aligned tabular columns, so a list reads down like a board.
- From the transit diagram: the chosen line burns brightest. Selection is the
  only saturated thing on a screen; everything at rest is ink and neutrals.
- From the developer console: destructive actions are isolated. Kept from
  0.23 (the More menu); brick is reserved for them.
Verdicts: transit diagram competitive (clarity), console declined, cassette
j-card declined, doujin catalog declined, botanical folio declined (cream
ground is an AI default here), split-flap competitive (density). None wins
both axes against the pinned direction.

roll's assignment), 7 sun-faded bus route boards. seed:5373fceb6c1581df
